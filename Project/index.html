What is this?

When I was younger, my introduction to music was done by my parents both replaying CD's of my favorite albums during car rides, and by ripping said CD's to their own computer so that it could be synced to a portable music device such as an iPod and Walkman.

As I grew up, I slowly put together a small library out of these ripped files so that I enjoy music offline on mobile. When I started to use PC's more, the desire to put together a full-fledged library slowly grew, out of frustration about using YouTube playlists for this matter and subscription-based models of streaming services pushing me away from using them.

This page serves to be a collection of resources I have used to set up multiple local music libraries in the past, all mainly for Windows and Linux on PC. No music files will also be handed out per page.

Anyone may use any information on this page to put together a similar site for this purpose. I may even put together a blog for this outside of here in the years to come, if the technology for hosting pages like that can still exist.
